TKMingle is a student collaboration and social discovery platform built exclusively for TKM College of Engineering (TKMCE), Kollam.

An independent student community initiative, not an official administrative release or trademark of the TKM College Trust.

Features:
- Cross-department student networking (breaking branch silos)
- Campus Circles for IEEE, IEDC, Coding Club, and RoboTKM
- Real-time peer direct messaging powered by open Supabase infrastructure
- Verified student profiles
