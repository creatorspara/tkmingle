SECTION 02 — iOS
PWA
iOS access is being developed as a Progressive Web App (PWA).
Why PWA?
  • No paid Apple Developer membership required for distribution through the web.
  • Accessible directly through Safari.
  • Can be added to the iPhone Home Screen seamlessly.
  • Provides an app-like experience without an App Store release.
A native iOS App Store release may be considered in the future.
